The Open Architecture Management (OAM) is the activity that manages, sustains, and evolves the Open Architecture Management Standards (OAMS). The OAM standards consists of the Universal Command and Control Interface standard (UCI) and the Open Mission Systems (OMS) standard. The OAMS enable current, legacy, and new programs to realize the benefits of Open Architecture.
